LATIN JOSEPHUS: BAMBERG IDENTITY
Selected by Richard M. Pollard, 2 October 2026.

Historical reference
Staatsbibliothek Bamberg, Msc.Class.78, fol. 2r.
Digital image viewer:
https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00140762?page=7
IIIF manifest:
https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb00140762/manifest

The red display capitals and the dotted stroke beneath IOSEPHI informed
the new outlined wordmark and its supporting motifs. The LI monogram is a
contemporary design for Latin / Iosephus; it is not a historical monogram.
The lettering is adapted rather than a facsimile. The palette consists of
chosen digital colours, not measured manuscript pigment values.

The primary IIIF manifest identifies the digitization with Public Domain
Mark 1.0. A syndicated catalogue record gives different terms. Retain the
manuscript credit and consult the complete provenance notes in the design
package when reproducing the source photograph.

This site overlay uses only the newly drawn design assets. It contains
no reproduction of the manuscript photograph and no Nonantola ornament.
The complete design package includes the reference images, exact source
metadata and fuller adaptation documentation. No blanket new licence for
the original design assets is declared here.
