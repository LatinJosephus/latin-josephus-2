# Source authority and transcription policy

The elected source is the main printed contents text of Benedictus Niese. Perseus current and legacy XML are separate digital witnesses and typing aids; canonical XML is the comparison witness. No silent replacement of print by digital text is authorized. No Loeb control was needed, and no Whiston source research was undertaken.

The four title pages were visually inspected. Their common imprint reads Flavii Iosephi Opera, edidit et apparatu critico instruxit Benedictus Niese; Berolini, apud Weidmannos. Dates and coverage below come from the scans, not filenames.

Current grc2 uses a contents `head` plus an `arg` section and numbered paragraphs. Legacy Hopper uses a title note containing a list, with tokens such as `[στιγμα]` for numeral six and `[χ1000 ]` for thousands. Legacy Book I puts the unnumbered proem rubric before the heading; print and current grc2 put it after. Both retain the same19 numbered entries. Current/legacy raw XML, line references and literal readings are preserved; neither interface is mistaken for an independent printed edition.

The digital header records a 2004-01-29 revision: “Tag tables of contents as NOTEs since they don't align to the English.” This documents treatment as paratext rather than aligned narrative. Its metadata also says 1885–1890 and names a Loeb Classical Library series; the scanned Niese title pages control actual bibliography, including volume III (1892). No metadata correction was written into the downloaded witness.

Exact retrieval URLs, dates/statuses where returned, response headers and SHA-256 values are in sources/DIGITAL_RETRIEVAL.json, sources/DIGITAL_RETRIEVAL_2.json and sources/OLD_HOPPER_RETRIEVAL.json. A failed chapter-zero route and a catalogue TLS limitation were recorded rather than bypassed; successful whole-book exports supply all ten comparison lists.

Canonical controls read: existing Greek V, XI and XIV, the current source-contents registry and the four specified certification/census files. Their exact hashes are in sources/TARGETED_INSPECTION_INPUTS.json and the full baseline. Historical statuses are not substituted for actual production at the current HEAD.

Unicode and layout policy:
- raw_source_recovery: Original unaltered PDFs with SHA-256 plus page PNGs are the raw print witness; print has no native Unicode sequence. Raw digital XML is preserved byte-for-byte.
- typing_aid: Perseus strings seeded transcription; each elected heading, label, entry and closing was checked visually against print. Only PDF locators came from extraction.
- glyph_policy: Printed theta type is represented as ϑ (U+03D1), numeral-six glyph as ϛ (U+03DB), superscript numeral stroke as ʹ (U+0374), Greek apostrophe as ᾽ (U+1FBD). No NFC/NFKC rewrite of elected output. Lexical final sigma is retained. Exact type shapes and accent placement remain recoverable in images.
- layout_policy: Unletterspace the spaced heading for readable transcription, join ordinary line-wrap hyphenation, retain one item per printed label. Significant cross-page word in X.6 and continuous editorial bracket group in II are documented. No source words, labels, ordering or punctuation are silently corrected.
- comparison_only: NFC and declared theta/apostrophe/stroke/stigma encodings may be compared equivalently; they never overwrite raw or elected readings. Substantive, accent/breathing, bracket-scope and punctuation differences remain separate.

A printed scan has no native Unicode character sequence. The elected UTF-8 transcription explicitly uses ϑ U+03D1 for the printed theta type, ϛ U+03DB for the numeral-six glyph, ʹ U+0374 for the numeral stroke and ᾽ U+1FBD for Greek apostrophe. These choices are declared, not silently normalized; source glyph shapes remain in the page images. NFKC is never applied to the elected corpus. Ordinary wrap hyphens and letter-spacing in headings are not lexical material; X.6's cross-page word is explicitly documented. Square brackets and punctuation remain in the elected text.

No contents numeral has been mapped to a modern Chapter, Subchapter, Niese coordinate, Bamberg division or Alignment unit. IDs in the proposals identify only independent printed-paratext entries. No clickable narrative links are proposed.
