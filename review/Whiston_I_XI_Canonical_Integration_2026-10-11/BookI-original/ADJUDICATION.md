## I.81

Alternatives: Before But (adopted); Before Moses (rejected: leaves the adversative in §80)

Decision and evidence: Canonical text has the inherited extra character ú before Nisan. The candidate was displaced to Moses. Lexundria begins But Moses; the preceding Egypt sentence and following Nisan/Xanthicus clause establish the same position. Greek §81 begins Μωυσῆς δὲ before Nisan, confirming the adversative transition.

Confidence: MEDIUM. Canonical spelling preserved. Boundary resolved; textual differences remain inherited.
