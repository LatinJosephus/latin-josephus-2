## 4. Scholarly boundary method

**First perform a complete READ-ONLY reconnaissance for both books.** Verify exact current XML/renderer/registry, printed authority, alignment topology, all visible numbered claims, inherited starts, heading/capitula content, nested markup and known gap/unclear/corr/notes. Keep book-specific audit evidence separately; one book's success is not the other's certification. Build a complete candidate identity register with 404 and 355 individual records.

For EVERY Greek identity:
- inspect the correct Niese printed page image and numeral; record volume, printed p., PDF image p., exact Greek start candidate and sufficient page evidence for later verification;
- distinguish a marginal number alongside a LINE from an unambiguous word-level boundary; use syntax, nearby Greek, and Loeb as independently attributed corroboration where necessary;
- check if the Greek current marker is displaced, missing at book opening or already correct; move/add only with print support, an adjudicated exact change and byte-reversible record;
- preserve Greek original text and apparatus entirely.

For EVERY Latin identity:
- align the complete Greek interval with Latin narrative, not merely the inherited heading or the first aligned paragraph;
- review left/right neighbours and whole contexts, including crossing existing `div2`, `floatingText` and `argument` boundaries;
- find the earliest defensible physical Latin counterpart, even when compressed, damaged, syntactically reattached, repeated, displaced or embedded mid-word-group; do not infer absence solely from lack of matching words at an expected offset;
- record literal Latin before/after candidate, stable paragraph XML ID, text/tail path, mixed-content code-point offset, raw UTF-8 position and complete-source hash; preserve punctuation, spacing, IDs and markup;
- qualify partial, absent-in-transcription, uncertain, or displaced correspondence separately, with causes explicitly undetermined unless evidence establishes them;
- retain traditional and Bamberg starts exactly: Niese internal milestone is a separate coordinate, not a replacement;
- preserve `num`/citation labels as visible manuscript or older edition data even when they do not define the executable Niese start.

Use sources and tools for locating, but do not certify from automatic alignment alone. Do not correct the transmitted text on philological grounds; SEGMENTATION ONLY. No supplementation, normalization, substitution, paragraph reordering or unrequested source-TOC reconstruction.
