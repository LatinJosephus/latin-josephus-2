# Minimal shared reader support

The pinned VIII/X reader already supports per-book identity registries, suppressed inherited claims, unavailable Latin selections with independent Greek/English context, and correspondence notices. XII uses that mechanism. The only added range behavior recognizes an explicit `milestone unit="niese-end"` when the final Latin section has no following start. This stops XII.434 before the unmarked, unchanged subscription. Book, traditional, Bamberg and Alignment views continue to preserve the subscription. No other book has that end marker.

The audit mapper projects documented plain division labels and subscriptions out of narrative coordinates while retaining full source-node offsets and raw-byte positions. Projection leaves corpus bytes intact. Per-book marker plans list these exclusions. The authored compact-review helper records individually examined observations; it does not derive correspondence automatically.

Reader certification must compare the actual new-book build with the frozen baseline across affected ranges and protected works. This note records the scope of the change and does not claim that certification has run.
