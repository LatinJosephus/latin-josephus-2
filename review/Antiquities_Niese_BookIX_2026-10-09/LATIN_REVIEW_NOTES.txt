# Individually read Greek/Latin correspondence. Format section|exact anchor|review observation.
1|Iosaphat itaque rege|Return, Jehu's rebuke, and reprieve open both accounts.
2|et tunc equidem|Thanksgiving precedes the circuit of the country in both accounts.
3|Constituens que iudices|Appointment and impartiality of local judges.
4|haec cum docuisset|Return to Jerusalem and appointment of its judges; preserve corrupt wording.
5|Si hoc de rebus|Consultations on greater disputes and capital-city justice.
6|horum autem principes|Appointment of Amasiah and Zebadiah and concluding summary.
7|Eo namque tempore|Moabite/Ammonite/Arab invasion and Engaddi setting.
8|Audiens autem iosaphat|News, assembly, and prayer for help.
9|dum et his|Builders' prayer for protection; syntactically subordinate Latin joins the Greek independent explanatory clause.
10|Quo facto quidam|Prophet enters the assembly and promises divine intervention.
11|eosque inuenirent|Location of the enemies and command to watch; Latin infinitive depends on preceding command.
12|Die siquidem facto|Dawn, Tekoa, and the king's orders.
13|placuit sententia|Approval and mutual slaughter of the enemies.
14|Iosaphat autem respiciens|View of the dead and order to take spoils.
15|diebus que tribus|Three-day collection and fourth-day blessing; Latin links to preceding plundering command.
16|exinde uero|Return, feasts, neighbouring fear, and reputation.
17|Fuit autem amicus|Partnership in ships and failed expedition; all repetitions preserved.
18|Achab filius|Ahaziah's reign and impiety; missing personal name in opening wording does not erase the section.
19|Cumque iam|Second regnal year, Moabite revolt, fall, and appeal to Ekron.
20|Apparuit que deus|God's direction to Elijah and message of death.
21|helię uero|Messengers return and explain their rapid return.
22|regi precipiente|Description of Elijah and dispatch of first officer.
23|Tribunus autem|First officer confronts Elijah and is consumed.
24|hanc perditione|Second officer and second destruction.
25|Agnoscens ohoc|Third officer's dispatch and courteous plea.
26|helias autem suscipiens|Elijah accepts and foretells death in the king's presence.
27|Et ille siquidem|Death and succession of Joram; account of impiety.
28|Erat enim|Other character description then Elijah/Enoch disappearance; preserve transmitted negative Latin versus Greek δραστήριος.
29|Suscipiens itaque|Moabite campaign and previous tribute.
30|Congregans ergo|Alliance requested from Jehoshaphat and Edom.
31|Ioram autem talibus|Reception in Jerusalem, route, and three kings' march.
32|et dum septem|Seven-day wandering and water shortage, complaint to God.
33|quem iosaphat confortabat|Jehoshaphat reassures Joram, seeks prophet, and visits Elisha.
34|dumque ad prophetam|Arrival at prophet's outside camp and request for prophecy.
35|Illo uero iurante|Oath, musician, inspiration, and digging pits.
36|dicens, neque nube|Prediction of water without rain and victory; speech introduction belongs to the prediction.
37|hec dicente propheta|Water appears at dawn.
38|Cumque audissent|Moabite defensive preparations upon hearing of advance.
39|Et cum non esset|Distance, sunrise, red water, and imagined blood; Latin moves distance ahead of seeing water.
40|Quodcum arbitrati|Request to loot, advance, and rout.
41|Tres ergo reges|Devastation of cities, fields, trees, waters, and walls.
42|Rex autem moabitarum|King's unsuccessful escape with seven hundred.
43|reuersus in ciuitatem|Return, sacrifice of heir, and compassionate withdrawal.
44|Iosaphat autem reuersus|Jehoshaphat's peaceful return, death, and burial.
45|Reliquid autem|Sons and succession of Joram.
46|ueniens autem|Israelite king's return with Elisha and narrative transition.
47|Ad hunc enim|Obadiah's widow and debt.
48|Qui dum requisisset|Oil inquiry and borrowed vessels.
49|que cum completa|Completed vessels and report; woman/children's obedience is compressed into the result.
50|Ille uero consilium|Sale and debt repayment; corrupt closing words preserved, then editorial placeholder excluded from narrative.
