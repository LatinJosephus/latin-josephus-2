## Adjudication

The original Toronto colour scan makes the clause legible; the Illinois copy independently confirms all words and punctuation. The 1856 punctuation is commas after THIS, ANGRY, YEARS, NOT, TIME and EGYPT, and a point after CANAAN. There is no comma after FORETOLD and no bracket pair around DURING THAT TIME.

The 1741 control instead has semicolons after this and angry, a comma after foretold, a colon after years, and literal brackets around during that time. The 1784 control has the earlier comma after foretold and brackets, but commas after angry and Years; some ink remains damaged. Chicago resembles the earlier punctuation; Gutenberg/canonical omit several 1856 commas. These witnesses are kept separate.

The earlier hold was a legibility problem in the examined rendition. The original colour scan is more legible than the processed PDF; another held copy confirms the reading. Uneven inking, scan contrast and derivative processing cannot be separated completely from these images, so no exclusive physical-damage diagnosis is asserted. No substantive CONTINUE-versus-another-word edition variant is established. The adopted 1856 string is unchanged; its qualification and unclear wrapper are superseded.
