# Source authority and provenance

Audit date: 8 October 2026. Identified printed page images govern the proposed printed transcription. Digital witnesses are collated independently. Current LatinJosephus XML remains a separate project witness. No witness is silently repaired.

## Printed witnesses

**1737 — bibliographical authority only.** William Whiston, translator, *The Genuine Works of Flavius Josephus, the Jewish Historian*. London: printed by William Bowyer for the author, 1737. ESTC T112661. Consulted [Soane b9618](https://collections.soane.org/b9618), [Eton B13330](https://catalogue.etoncollege.com/B13330) and [Google catalogue](https://books.google.com/books?id=FPI7zwEACAAJ). Soane records extensive preliminary pagination, 1,021 main pages and 83 final unnumbered pages; its copy has known missing leaves. Eton records a two-volume folio copy. These records establish publication and collation, not exact heading wording or arrangement. Page-image access was not obtained. The advertised “Contents” remains NOT_YET_DETERMINED.

**1741 — limited early printed control.** *The Genuine Works of Flavius Josephus, the Jewish Historian*, translated by William Whiston, Vol. I. Dublin: printed by R. Reilly for George Ewing, at the Angel & Bible in Dame’s Street, 1741. [Google Books, ID 6mwoL_GaNu4C](https://books.google.ca/books?id=6mwoL_GaNu4C&printsec=frontcover). Digital metadata identifies the British Library copy, digitized 4 May 2017. Title page PP9 was visually checked. Antiquities Book I/Preface starts at p. 285; I.1 is at p. 290; Book III ends at p. 466. Vol. I thus covers I–III. Page 290 prints its chapter summary in italics and includes a footnote concerning chapter titles wanting in the best manuscripts. This is not proof of an independent contents list. Textual identity with 1737 is not asserted merely because this is a later Whiston printing.

Title image sources/google1741_PP9.jpg: SHA-256 **f6e5cdc6b42381d84abb46d81dc65b6d518f514d05e3005923aa3a7cb84d4dc9**. Verified control images and viewer provenance are recorded in GOOGLE1741_CONTROL_DOWNLOADS.json and SOURCE_HASHES.json. Failed PDF downloads, an HTML response saved with an .epub suffix, and access-placeholder images are archived research responses, **not valid printed-source evidence**.

**1784 — partial earlier print control.** *The Genuine Works of Flavius Josephus, the Jewish Historian*, translated by William Whiston, in two volumes, Vol. I. Newcastle upon Tyne: printed by and for L. Dinsdale and T. Angus, 1784. [Internet Archive dli.bengal.10689.4952](https://archive.org/details/dli.bengal.10689.4952); Uttarpara Jaykrishna Public Library, Hooghly; DLI/C-DAC Kolkata digitization. The title at PDF page 2 was visually identified. The 714-page scan includes Antiquities I–XX, but XX.11 is cut off after a catchword on printed p. 706 / PDF page 713, followed by a blank image. Faded print and the incomplete ending prevent its adoption as a complete governing copy here. V.3 at printed p. 152 / PDF page 162 reads “them”.

sources/Whiston1784_VolI.pdf: SHA-256 **0ca21fc696f668334c16d01b83b8277fd4b16d465294543f39056baa20360dd4**. OCR, scan metadata and locating experiments are archived; OCR locating is not promoted to visual certification.

**1856 — governing candidate transcription.** *The Works of Flavius Josephus, the Learned and Authentic Jewish Historian*, translated by William Whiston, with three dissertations and a complete index, complete in one volume. Auburn and Rochester: Alden & Beardsley, 1856. [University of Toronto/Kelly Library scan, Internet Archive worksofflaviusj00joseuoft](https://archive.org/details/worksofflaviusj00joseuoft), call number BDG7417. Title: PDF page 11; scan: 900 pages. Antiquities Preface: printed pp. 27–29 / PDF pages 39–41; Books I–XX: printed p. 29 / PDF page 41 through p. 550 / PDF page 562. There are 256 running chapter headings. The publisher is identified; a separately verified printer imprint is not invented.

- sources/Whiston1856.pdf: SHA-256 **2753e37ffc9567d4f10a079bc7f7e11d10386fa16c31167503a89c34a038e912**.
- sources/whiston1856_djvu.xml: SHA-256 **8d41355c99d781b829a8caad305b6007e307f57ab5b38d7820a33010a7f42ef7**.
- sources/whiston1856_scandata.xml: SHA-256 **2f574321c22cb2c1a074ef520508247c7af6dc411927721fba1f0e4bb810d79a**.

Each heading has a printed-page coordinate, PDF/image locator and visual record in the master and VISUAL_INSPECTION_REGISTER.json. PDF pages are one-based; IA leaf numbers are zero-based. In the Antiquities sequence, printed page equals PDF page minus 12, checked against visible running pagination at inspected pages. Faded page numerals remain recoverable in images. Small capitals are represented by uppercase Unicode; physical wrapping and type remain available in crops. Ordinary wrap hyphens are joined explicitly; lexical hyphens and ligatures remain. This is an edition-identified transcription, not a photographic diplomatic replacement.

A 1792 catalogue/microfilm family was investigated; OCR title material appears to say 1793. Without title-image verification it was not promoted to a securely dated print witness. Maynard and undated later-source leads are excluded from the eighteenth-century Whiston authority claim.
