# Pietro Lauro (1544) — Italian DEH

Pietro Lauro’s Italian translation of _De excidio Hierosolymitano_ was printed at Venice by Michel Tramezino in 1544, under the title _Historia d’Egesippo_. The printed title describes it as translated from Latin into Italian.

## Edition

_Historia d’Egesippo tra i christiani scrittori antichissimo de le valorose imprese fatte da giudei ne l’assedio di Gierusaleme…_, trans. Pietro Lauro (Venice: Michel Tramezino, 1544). EDIT16 CNCE 35123.

## Comparison with the English translation

Comparison with Lauro’s Italian translation of 1544 led to three revisions of our English, two adopting his interpretation. Across 216 Ussani sections, the audit recorded 308 cases where his rendering was judged less persuasive or freer, together with six further cases warranting reconsideration of our English.

## Transcription and editorial conventions

Historical spelling, accents, abbreviations, **ß** and the line-break marker **¬** remain in the source data. Long s is displayed as ordinary **s** throughout the reader; the downloadable data retain the original characters. The flowing reading view joins physical word breaks for display only. **Source lines** and **Folio** show the physical line divisions and break markers.

On **f. 137r**, **apprestos[si]** includes an editorially supplied ending. The brackets distinguish the supplied letters. Original readings and all 199 saved editorial decisions remain recoverable in the project’s audit checkpoints.

Folio labels count each recto and verso separately: image 1 is f. 1r, image 2 is f. 1v, image 3 is f. 2r, and image 417 is f. 209r.

Source matter outside the translated narrative is retained separately from the aligned running text. Deferred geometry proposals have not been treated as textual corrections or approved field revisions. Images 418–438 are outside this text’s scope.
