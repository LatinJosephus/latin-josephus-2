# Pietro Lauro (1544) — Italian DEH

Pietro Lauro’s Italian translation of _De excidio Hierosolymitano_ was printed at Venice by Michel Tramezino in 1544, under the title _Historia d’Egesippo_. The printed title describes it as translated from Latin into Italian.

## Edition

_Historia d’Egesippo tra i christiani scrittori antichissimo de le valorose imprese fatte da giudei ne l’assedio di Gierusaleme…_, trans. Pietro Lauro (Venice: Michel Tramezino, 1544). EDIT16 CNCE 35123.

## Comparison with the English translation

Comparison with Lauro’s Italian translation of 1544 led to three revisions of our English, two adopting his interpretation. Across 216 Ussani sections, the audit recorded 308 cases where his rendering was judged less persuasive or freer, together with six further cases warranting reconsideration of our English.
