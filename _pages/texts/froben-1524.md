# Johann Froben (1524) — Latin Antiquities

Johann Froben printed the Latin _Antiquities_ at Basel in September 1524. The project presents the Proem and all twenty books from the copy held by Universitätsbibliothek Basel (UBH FQ I 5).

## Reading and searching Froben

In the reader, Froben can be read alongside the [Bamberg text]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/latin-antiquities/). The Froben pane can follow the reader’s passage selection or be browsed by the edition’s own printed divisions and pages. Search results are grouped by printed page and link to that page in the reader.
