# Johann Froben (1524) — Latin _Bellum Judaicum_

This transcription of Josephus’s Latin _Bellum Judaicum_ covers the prologue and all seven books in Johann Froben’s Basel edition. It is based on the copy at the Universitätsbibliothek Basel (UBH FQ I 5). [Basel catalogue record and facsimile](https://www.e-rara.ch/bau_1/content/titleinfo/27897140).

[Read Froben alongside Cardwell]({{site.baseurl}}/bellum-judaicum/?book=1&niese=1&froben=1) · [Open Froben’s printed Book VII]({{site.baseurl}}/bellum-judaicum/?book=6&froben=1&froben-view=book&froben-location=froben-book-7).

## Reading Froben

In the Froben pane, **Text · current reading view** follows the reader’s book, chapter, subchapter, or Niese section. The reader uses the Greek book divisions followed by [Cardwell’s edition]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/latin-bellum-judaicum/).

The printed book and division controls follow Froben’s traditional Latin divisions. The scan page view includes printed marginalia. **Show printed page labels** adds links to the corresponding Basel scans.

## Transcription and alignment

The 260 pages were transcribed in Transkribus and proofread against the Basel scans. Cardwell supplied a comparison for proofreading and alignment to Niese sections. The transcription follows Froben’s readings.

Flavius Josephus, _Flavii Iosephi, patria Hierosolymitani, religione Iudaei, inter Graecos historiographos, cum primis facundi, opera quaedam_ (Basel, Johann Froben, 1524). See also [Froben’s _Antiquitates Judaicae_]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/froben-1524/).
