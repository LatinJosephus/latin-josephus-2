# Latin Contra Apionem

The Latin _Contra Apionem_ presented here uses Karl Boysen’s edition of 1898. R.M. Pollard prepared the electronic edition in 2026.

## Source and references

Boysen’s _Flavii Iosephi opera ex versione Latina antiqua. Pars VI: De Iudaeorum vetustate sive Contra Apionem libri II_ was published as CSEL 37 (Vienna, Prague and Leipzig, 1898).

[View Boysen’s printed edition](https://archive.org/details/flaviilosephiope37joseuoft) · [Read the Contra Apionem]({{site.baseurl}}/contra-apionem/).

The two books use Niese section references: I.1–320 and II.1–296. Chapter divisions and Niese sections are represented separately in the reader.

## English translations

The reader offers [Whiston]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/whiston/) and [Lodge]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/lodge/). Their shared discussion pages also cover the _Antiquities_ and the _Jewish War_.
