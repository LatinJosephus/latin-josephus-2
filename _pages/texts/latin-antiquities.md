# Latin Antiquities

The Latin _Antiquities_ presented here is based on Bamberg Msc.Class.78. The complete text, including the Proem and twenty books, was published by the project in 2019 and revised in 2026.

## The project’s edition

The text was prepared by R.M. Pollard, J. Timmermann, J. di Gregorio, M. Laprade and J.-F. Aubé-Pronce. The reader brings together the Latin, the Greek text and English translations, with Niese references and manuscript divisions.

[Read the Antiquities]({{site.baseurl}}/antiquities/) · [View the manuscript catalogue]({{site.baseurl}}/manuscripts/).

## Editions and manuscript source

- [Bamberg Msc.Class.78, digitised manuscript](https://zendsbb.digitale-sammlungen.de/db/0000/sbb00000114/images/).
- Franz Blatt, ed., _The Latin Josephus. I: Introduction and Text. The Antiquities: Books I–V_ (Aarhus, 1958).
- Randolf Lukas, ed., [*Josephus Latinus, Antiquitates Judaicae. Buch 6 und 7*](https://www.wvttrier.de/p/josephus-latinus-antiquitates-judaicae-buch-6-und-7) (Trier, 2022), a critical edition of Books VI–VII.

## English translations

The reader offers [Whiston]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/whiston/) and [Lodge]({{site.baseurl}}/texts/lodge/). Each translator has a shared discussion page covering all three Josephus works available on this site.
