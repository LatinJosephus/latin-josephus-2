# Jean Millet (1556) — French DEH

Jean Millet, of Saint-Amour, translated _De excidio Hierosolymitano_ from Latin into French. His _Les cinq livres de l’histoire d’Egesippe, contenans plusieurs guerres des Iuifs, & la ruine de Hierusalem_ was published at Paris by Guillaume Cavellat in 1556. The colophon names Benoist Prévost as printer and gives the date 17 January 1556.

[Universal Short Title Catalogue — USTC 14879](https://ustc.ac.uk/editions/14879).

## The project’s transcription

The site preserves Millet’s historical wording, printed chapters, folios and preliminary matter. Recorded passage correspondences connect the French with the modern Ussani Latin text. Ussani’s edition provides a comparison framework. Millet, of course, would have used the *editio princeps* published by D'Étaples and Hummelberg in [1510](https://books.google.ca/books?id=sBZSAAAAcAAJ), or one of its reprints from [1511](https://books.google.ca/books?id=Up8eEuE3TYIC) or [1524](https://books.google.ca/books?id=2K77lcNroW0C).
