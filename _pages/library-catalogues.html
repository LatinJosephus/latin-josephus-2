Josephus and Hegesippus in library catalogues to c. 1000

Entries for Josephus and Hegesippus in early medieval booklists, inventories and records of gifts.

Dates refer to the lists and include ranges that cross 1000. The Latin quotations follow the editions cited.

MBKDS stands for Mittelalterliche Bibliothekskataloge Deutschlands und der Schweiz and MBKÖ for Mittelalterliche Bibliothekskataloge Österreichs. Häse (2002) refers to Mittelalterliche Bücherverzeichnisse aus Kloster Lorsch.

Doubtful identification

Passau, 8 September 903. The entry includes enigmata Simphosii et Althelmi et Ioseppi. Manitius (1907), p. 663, identifies Ioseppus as Joseph Scottus. Dorofeeva (2023), p. 60, proposes the Hypomnesticon of Josephus Christianus. Ineichen-Eder, MBKDS 4.1, 26.65–66. Dorofeeva, Reading Nature in the Early Middle Ages.
