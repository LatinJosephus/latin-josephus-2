Text and scope

Currently indexed: Cardwell’s Latin main text of all seven books of Bellum Judaicum, including the proem of Book I. Antiquities will be added after its text repairs and verification are complete. Editorial titles, printed division labels and manuscript annotations are excluded.

Every word remains searchable. Spelling is preserved in the excerpts; the optional equivalence setting affects matching only. No grammatical stemming or automatic spelling correction is applied.

Within each section: every Boolean condition and exact phrase is evaluated in that section.

Whole book: Boolean conditions may be satisfied anywhere in the same book; exact phrases must still be consecutive and may cross Niese or chapter boundaries. Each book, work and edition is evaluated separately. A phrase never continues into another book. Result excerpts identify the sections where positive terms occur.

Passages: results follow the order of the text. A section with several matches appears once in section scope. In whole-book scope, a phrase spanning sections can have its own excerpt. Click a citation to open the corresponding section in the reader.

Concordance: each positive word or phrase match has its own row, with up to ten words of context on each side. Original spelling is retained. A phrase spanning sections appears in one row.

Counts: a single word, wildcard or quoted phrase has an occurrence count as well as a count of matching sections or books. Boolean queries count matching sections or books; concordance rows show their positive matches and are not word frequencies. A section or book can satisfy a negative condition without a word to highlight.

Download all results: the CSV includes every concordance row and the citations of any matching sections or books without positive matches, regardless of which view or page is displayed. It records the completed query, its settings, the edition and links to the reader. Changes typed into the search box take effect after you press Search.
