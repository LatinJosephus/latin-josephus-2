# DEH Pollard Millet editorial decisions of 25 October 2026

Approved revision 1.1 implements eight running-text revisions and eight annotation-only decisions. Canonical Latin, Millet and the historical v1.0 publication remain unchanged.

## A1 I.32.6 REVISED

Canonical unit: `latin-deh1-num124`. English unit: `english-deh1-num124`.

Latin lemma: Quid seruatur post terrae motum ac pestilentiam nisi misericordia, quia soluimus duplicia peccata?

Previous English: What remains after earthquake and pestilence except mercy, since we have paid for twofold sins?

Revised English: What remains after earthquake and pestilence but mercy, since we have paid twice over for our sins?

Approved transferred or metonymic interpretation of punishment already exacted. Duplicia grammatically modifies peccata; that grammatical attachment remains recorded and the Latin is unchanged.

## A2 I.42.2 REVISED

Canonical unit: `latin-deh1-num178`. English unit: `english-deh1-num178`.

Latin lemma: Herodias quoque, et Mariammes filiae, quas sexus non inpediret et regni cupido exagitaret

Previous English: Aristobulus was survived by Herod, Agrippa and Aristobulus, born of Berenice, Salome’s daughter. Herodias too survived, and Mariamne’s daughters, whom their sex would not hinder and desire for the kingdom would stir.

Revised English: Aristobulus was survived by Herod, Agrippa and Aristobulus, born of Berenice, Salome’s daughter, and by two daughters, Herodias and Mariamne, whom their sex would not hinder and desire for the kingdom would stir.

Approved identification of Herodias and Mariamne as the two named daughters of Aristobulus. The unusual canonical name-form Mariammes is preserved without Latin emendation.

## A3 I.44.1 REVISED

Canonical unit: `latin-deh1-num194`. English unit: `english-deh1-num194`.

Latin lemma: Obstare itaque eos suis utilitatibus Antipater arbitrabatur atque incumbere suis artibus, ut spe adulescentium simulata omnia factionis suae studia obumbrarentur

Previous English: Antipater therefore thought that they stood in the way of his interests and pressed upon his devices, so that the hope placed in the young men would overshadow all the feigned efforts of his intrigue.

Revised English: Antipater therefore thought that they stood in the way of his interests, and set to work with his own stratagems, so that by pretending to place his hopes in the young men he might conceal all the efforts of his faction.

Antipater resumes as the understood subject of incumbere; spe adulescentium simulata expresses his pretended hopes, concealing his factional activity. The following fabricated letters remain unchanged.

## A5 II.10.5 REVISED

Canonical unit: `latin-deh2-num38`. English unit: `english-deh2-num38`.

Latin lemma: Octauus sollemnitatis dies, qua ligna omnes altaribus solebant inponere

Previous English: The eighth day of the festival, at which everyone was accustomed to lay wood on the altars so that the fire should never fail—it had to remain unextinguished—increased their frenzy, so that all the attendants were excluded from the temple.

Revised English: On the eighth day, a festival day when everyone was accustomed to lay wood on the altars so that the fire should never fail—it had to remain unextinguished—the occasion increased their frenzy, so that all the attendants were excluded from the temple.

The eighth day follows the preceding seven days of fighting; no eight-day wood-offering festival is implied. The approved adverbial opening requires a main-clause subject, so the occasion is supplied; the fire qualification and remainder of the sentence are retained.

## A6 IV.26.1 REVISED

## B1 I.10.3 ANNOTATED

Canonical unit: `latin-deh1-num31`. English unit: `english-deh1-num31`.

Latin lemma: Cuius uictoria libertate adsuetis dominationis metum mouebat

Exact annotation: Translation comparison: The antecedent of cuius is not explicitly named: Pollard understands Demetrius, while Alexander’s recovered ascendancy is another possible referent. Millet recasts the sentence and does not unambiguously resolve the antecedent.

Approved annotation-only decision; running English retained exactly. This is translation commentary, not a textual variant.
